UNDERSTANDING TYPE 2 DIABETES

Living With Type 2 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes is a long-term condition that requires ongoing, personalised care. Good diabetes care combines understanding the condition with healthy living, blood glucose management, medicines when needed, and regular review with the healthcare team.

🩸

Blood Glucose Management

HbA1c is used to monitor blood glucose control, and targets should be agreed according to the person's individual circumstances.

🥗

Healthy Living

Dietary advice and physical activity are discussed alongside other parts of a personalised diabetes management plan.

💊

Medicines & Treatment

Medicine choices are individualised, taking account of benefits, risks, other health conditions and the person's preferences.

❤️

Ongoing Care

Diabetes care includes education, regular reviews and attention to diabetes-related complications and individual needs.

Based on NICE NG28: Type 2 diabetes in adults: management.
