About Type 2 Diabetes

What Type 2 diabetes is, and how T2D Care uses NICE guidance to support your understanding.

What is Type 2 diabetes?

Type 2 diabetes is a long-term condition in which the body does not use insulin properly, leading to higher-than-normal blood glucose (sugar) levels. Over time, unmanaged blood glucose can affect the heart, kidneys, eyes, nerves, and feet, which is why ongoing, individualised care is important.

How T2D Care works

T2D Care is an educational tool. Its AI Assistant answers questions using content drawn directly from the NICE guideline NG28 — Type 2 diabetes in adults: management . It is designed to help you understand recommendations, not to diagnose you or replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Important

T2D Care does not prescribe or recommend specific medicines or dosages. Any decisions about starting, stopping, or changing medication should always be made with a doctor or another qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, seek immediate medical care.
